# What the launch film taught the harness (2026-09-05)

Source: the whole StudyPDF launch-film session (about 100 rounds of feedback, two formats,
three music passes). Every item below is a moment where the harness cost time or where a
check that could have existed did not. Ordered by how often it hurt.

## 1. One command per edit round: `mh check`

Today a content edit means: typecheck, `scripts/cursor-targets.ts` (per format!), `mh doctor`,
`mh frames --sheet` for the touched scenes, `mh render`. Chained by hand every round, and the
cursor step was forgotten more than once.

- `mh check [--scene a,b]` = typecheck, doctor, re-measure declared cursor targets for EVERY
  format, lint (static + timeline + rendered on the touched scenes), sheets for the touched scenes.
- Cursor targets become harness data: `cursor: { legs: [...] }` in the timeline, measured by the
  DOM probe at render time, per format. No generated `cursor-targets*.ts`, no stub-file bootstrap
  bug, no "vertical had no cursor at all" for a whole day.
- Event literals live in one place. Today `PROBE_CLICKS` lives in the component and is mirrored
  into `harness.config.ts` `EVENTS`. The timeline should be the source and the component reads it.

## 2. Format parity is a lint, not a favour

The 9:16 film was never looked at until Luis asked. Then: cards clipped left and right, phones
too small under the wordmark, Bo on the wrong side, text wrapping onto three lines, mark on top
of the line. Every one of those is measurable from the probe.

- `overflow`: any probed element whose box leaves the format frame (both formats, every scene).
- `wrap`: text elements whose ink rect is taller than `lineHeight * declaredLines` (headline
  wrapped to a third line, label broke onto two).
- `collision`: two probed elements overlapping (mark vs line, chip vs chip at `drop+23`).
- `same-top`: demo scenes must share the stage top margin per format (house rule that was
  broken by `exam` and only noticed by a subagent).
- `format-parity`: a scene with cursor legs must show the cursor in every format; probes
  declared for a scene must be visible in every format.
- `lint --rendered` runs on both formats by default.

## 3. Audio needs ears, not only a meter

Found by ear or by hand-run ffmpeg: a "hit" that was a 3 s riser, a trimmed loop that went
silent for 6 s, a bed that ended mid-scene, a film 1.5 s longer than its music, a click bed
under the phone that did not belong there.

- `mh audio` checks every sfx file once: duration, attack time (time to peak), tail. Warn when a
  cue named like a hit has an attack over 150 ms or a length over 1.5 s.
- Music coverage: warn when a non-looped bed ends before the film, when a loop seam falls inside
  a scene (print the scene), and when the film is longer than the bed by more than a second.
- Print the music's own loud span (first and last second above a threshold) so `trim` is chosen
  from numbers, not guessed. This session guessed 2 to 54 for a track that is loud until 59.
- `mh audio --scene today` prints every cue that lands in that scene, so "what is ticking under
  the phone" is one command.

## 4. The reviewer's words must land on frames without a translator

Luis writes "bei 1:09", "bild 2", "die Topic Map", "beim Klicken". `mh resolve` handles the
first form, the agent still has to spot the timestamp and call it, and a screenshot only Luis has
cannot be resolved at all.

- `mh feedback --from text.md`: parse free text, resolve every timestamp, scene name and
  event name it contains, and print the addressed list. Unresolvable phrases are listed too.
- `mh locate screenshot.png`: match a pasted still against the last frames run (perceptual
  hash) and answer with `scene+frame`. Luis pastes frames every round.
- The review player already produces scene addresses. Make it the delivery channel: `mh render
  --serve` prints a URL that survives re-renders, so the 30 MB upload limit stops mattering.

## 5. Sheets that do not lie

Two false positives came from tile borders, one from a sheet that skipped the exact frames of a
gesture, two from tiles too small to see a cursor.

- Sample density from events: the window `event-6 .. event+18` is always included at 2 f steps.
- `--zoom key`: crop tiles around a probed element (the cursor target) at 1:1 pixels.
- No hard tile borders; timestamp burnt into the tile, not the frame edge.
- `mh diff` against a tagged "approved" run after every render, and print the touched scenes
  first. Dead space over a card survived a 40-frame manual look and would not survive a diff.

## 6. Turnaround

A full render with audio and web copy is 100 to 190 s; one scene fix costs the whole thing.

- `mh render --scene x --preview`: render only that scene with its cues mixed, as a 5 s clip.
- Audio as a separate always-remixable track: a music change must never re-render picture.
  Today the mix is already separate; `--remix` should skip everything but the mix.
- Bundle once per edit, share it between frames, probe, motion and render (it is rebuilt
  per command now).

## 7. Doctor should know the environment

- ffmpeg filter availability (`drawtext` missing broke the first sheet), Remotion peer versions
  (the zod mismatch warning), composition ids registered in Root (a parallel session wiped them
  and the first signal was a render error), every cue file present and decodable, `.gitignore`
  hits for assets the film depends on (era clips and sfx landed in ignored folders twice).

## 8. Small but repeated

- Frame files named by scene-local address (`probe+14.png`), not part frame.
- `mh resolve` defaults to `--json` when piped.
- `beats --suggest` must re-run the timeline rules on its own suggestion.
- `--format all` for frames, lint, probe and render.
- A `clean` command for `.harness`.
- Concurrent sessions: a lock file under `.harness` with the owning session id, and a warning
  when the config or a composition changed on disk since the last bundle.

## What worked and must stay

`doctor` drift check, `resolve` for seconds, `lint --rendered` (found the wrong gold and 12
more colours in one pass after hours of eyeballing), `motion` (declared 12 f enter, measured
23 f), `beats` (13 of 34 cuts off grid), the review player's scene-addressed comments, segment
caching (0 cached, 35 rendered only when something changed).
